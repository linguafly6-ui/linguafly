# Linguafly B1 Editorial Guide
## Cambridge English B1 Preliminary alignment

This guide governs the editorial review and publication of English B1 academy units.

### Learner profile and level
- Target CEFR: B1 (independent user), not B2.
- Learners should understand the main points of clear standard input on familiar matters and handle common situations involving work, study, travel and everyday life.
- Use familiar, concrete contexts first; introduce less familiar ideas only with clear support.
- Keep instructions short, direct and accessible. Spanish support may clarify task instructions, but the language being practised remains natural English.

### Cambridge B1 Preliminary task alignment
Build practice that develops the skills and task types commonly assessed in B1 Preliminary:
- Reading: short messages, notices, emails, articles, reviews and longer factual or narrative texts; gist, detail, purpose, attitude and inference at B1.
- Writing: emails and articles/stories with a clear communicative purpose, appropriate register, organisation and linking.
- Listening: short conversations, announcements, interviews and discussions; identify gist, specific information, attitude and opinion.
- Speaking: personal information, preferences, describing experiences, giving reasons, comparing options and reaching a simple decision.
- Language: everyday grammar, vocabulary, collocations and functional expressions in meaningful contexts.

### Language syllabus
Prioritise B1 control and range:
- present and past simple/continuous; present perfect simple and continuous in familiar uses;
- future forms for plans, predictions and arrangements;
- first and second conditionals; basic time clauses;
- passive forms in common tenses; reported statements and questions in familiar contexts;
- defining relative clauses; modals for obligation, advice, possibility and permission;
- gerunds and infinitives; comparatives and superlatives; articles, quantifiers and prepositions;
- phrasal verbs, word formation, common collocations and topic vocabulary.

Do not introduce advanced C1/C2 structures as required knowledge. Stretch items must remain solvable with B1 language and context.

### Exercise quality
- Every published auto-marked exercise must have one defensible correct answer, matching options and a verified answer key.
- Distractors must be plausible, grammatically parallel where possible, and clearly incorrect in context.
- Never invent an answer key from a guess. Preserve source answer keys and verify option-key mapping.
- Avoid duplicate prompts, repeated distractors, unnatural sentences, ambiguous blanks and culturally obscure assumptions.
- Cloze items must provide enough context for a unique answer. Key word transformations must preserve meaning and obey the task's word limit.
- Reading questions must be answerable from the supplied text. Listening questions must have an actual transcript/audio source; do not label reading text as recorded audio.
- Speaking and writing prompts need clear purpose, audience and expected response length, with a simple B1 assessment rubric rather than fabricated single-answer grading.

### Editorial structure and progression
- Each unit should have a clear topic, measurable learning outcomes and a coherent progression from controlled practice to contextual use.
- Balance grammar, vocabulary, reading and listening across the course. Include speaking/writing production as supported tasks, not as fake multiple-choice substitutes.
- Recycle language from earlier units while adding a manageable new focus.
- Prefer a smaller complete unit with reliable correction over a larger unit padded with weak or repetitive exercises.

### Publication gate
A unit may be marked published only when:
1. its title and learning outcomes accurately describe the actual source content;
2. its published sections contain learner-ready tasks;
3. every auto-marked exercise has valid options and a verified answer key;
4. the correct answer can be graded through the production API;
5. the unit has been checked in the production learner view.

Draft units remain hidden from learners until these checks pass. Publishing must never reset, delete or rewrite learner progress.
